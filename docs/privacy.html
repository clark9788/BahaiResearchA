Privacy Policy for Bahá'í Research

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Bahá'í Research is an offline Android application for searching and viewing a bundled corpus of Bahá'í writings. It is provided by William Clark as a personal, non-commercial project.

Personal initiative

Bahá'í Research is a personal study and research project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Bahá'í International Community, the Universal House of Justice, any National Spiritual Assembly, or any other institution of the Bahá'í Faith.

Data collection and sharing

Bahá'í Research does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other tracking services.

Search terms, filters, and viewed passages remain on the device and are not sent to the publisher or to any server by the app. The app stores its bundled corpus and local application data in Android app storage. Android may independently back up or manage app data according to the device owner's settings and Google's services; this app does not receive that data.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores your reading text size and the number of passages a search returns in local app storage. These values are used only to make the app work as you configured it and are not transmitted anywhere.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes
