Author and title attribution

Each result shows the author and title of the source document, and its citation includes the paragraph or page locator when the source provides one. Copyright in the texts remains with the Bahá'í International Community and the respective rights holders; the attribution shown in search results does not transfer any rights.

Compilations

Some documents are compilations and appear with the author Compilation. The passages in a compilation are drawn from the original writings, and the underlying document from the Bahá'í Reference Library credits the original author and source where they are named.

To see that original context, long-press a result and choose Open source. The source viewer opens the passage in the original document as published on bahai.org/library, including the original author attribution wherever the source provides it.

Offline use and privacy

Bahá'í Research works entirely offline. The corpus is stored on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, and there are no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
