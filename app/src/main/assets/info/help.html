How to use Bahá'í Research

Offline search of the Bahá'í writings

Search with one to three key words

Enter one to three key words for the most exact results — for example best beloved justice.

  • One to three words are all required, which keeps the results precise.
  • With more than three words, the first three stay required and the remaining words become optional, which brings back broader results.
  • Press Search, or the search key on the keyboard.

Filter by author and title

Choose an author to limit results to that author. Then choose a title to narrow the search to a single work. All Authors and All Titles search the whole corpus. Changing the author resets the title filter.

Read the results

Results are ranked by relevance. Each card shows the passage, then its author and title. Long-press a card for the actions below.

Choose how many results

Settings in the ⋮ menu controls how many passages a search returns. Choose between 3 and 50; the default is 12.

Change the text size

Pinch with two fingers over the results to make the text larger or smaller. Your choice is remembered and also applies to the source viewer.

Long-press a result

  • Copy passage — copies only the text of the passage.
  • Copy with citation — copies the passage together with its author and title.
  • Open source — opens the passage in the original source document from bahai.org/library.

Compilations

When the author shows Compilation, the passage comes from a compiled work. Long-press the result and choose Open source to read it in the original document, where the original author and source are credited.

Bahá'í Research works fully offline. The app does not collect or transmit personal information.
